# Catalog backlog (after catalog v2: 0 blocker/high left)

Medium/low findings from the final merchandiser review against the live Outmate/Statafelshop data.

- [medium] The beschermplaat add-on on statafelrok-stretch offers one plate size per skirt range. The Ø80-85 cm skirt always offers the Ø78 cm plate, which is 7 cm too small on an Ø85 top. That includes our own Ø85 statafel-inklapbaar-professional, statafel-met-kruisvoet and terrastafel-rond-inklapbaar, and Outmate's main round statafels are Ø85 too. The Ø60-70 cm skirt always offers the Ø68 cm plate, which overhangs an Ø60/65 top. validate.py misses this because _diam() skips add-ons with a single size. The theme shows the variant title, so the error is visible but still wrong for part of the buyers. The table PDPs and the packs pick the right plate. Fix: give both rows (Ø78 and Ø83; Ø58/68), labelled per tabletop size, as the contract allows since round 5. Then extend check_addons so a skirt range must offer a plate for every tabletop size in that range.
- [medium] Printed walls come in one type only. bedrukte-zijwand-easy-up is a closed polyester wall, 2–8 m, printed on the outside or both sides. Missing: a printed heavy-duty PVC wall, printed walls with window or door, a printed half wall and a printed wall set. Outmate offers full colour on about 95 SKUs, including heavy-duty PVC walls 3–8 m (€229–1,035), half walls and wall sets (polyester 3×4,5 €825, 4×8 €1,799; heavy-duty PVC 3×3 €1,250). Knock-on effects on our heavy-duty PVC printed tent (€1,079–3,819): (a) the 'Dak en luifel' variants have no wall add-on at all; (b) the 'Dak, luifel en 3 zijwanden' variants in both cloths offer no fourth wall or door wall, so a closed printed tent cannot be ordered. We already print heavy-duty PVC walls inside that bundle, so selling them loose is a data and price task. Fix: add Uitvoering Polyester / Heavy duty PVC and the types Dicht / Met raam / Met deur to the printed wall, plus a printed wall-set variant. Wire it into the cloth-matched add-on lists.
- [medium] There are no trolley packs at the entry or horeca end. (1) No HDPE statafel + kar pack, although the HDPE Ø80 (€42.95) is the 'vanaf' anchor of the statafel collection. Outmate's HDPE Ø80 pack of 12 tables plus trolley is its cheapest pack (€879–915), and Statafelshop sells 11 HDPE tables with its small kar for €1,241.50. Our HDPE PDP only offers the 'Klein, voor 5 statafels' kar as a single add-on, with no pack discount and no listing under Pakketten. (2) No square or rectangular stackable statafel + kar pack. Outmate sells packs of 10–12 square or 120×70 horeca statafels with a trolley for €1,969–3,465. We offer the plateau kar only as an add-on. Fix: add 'pakket-statafels-hdpe-met-transportkar' (for example 6 and 12 pieces, colour × count) and 'pakket-statafels-vierkant-met-plateaukar' (5 or 10, size × decor) at 10–12% under the parts. Confirm how many HDPE tables each kar holds.
- [low] Stretch skirt price position on the category's top-volume item. Our 'Zonder topcover' at €15.95 is above Outmate's €14.99 skirt that includes a topcover (190 g/m²), and our 'Met topcover' at €19.95 is 33% above it. Statafelshop is €33.82 with topcover. We sit inside the market band and the 220 g/m² cloth and 24 colours justify it; kenmerk_kort already shows 220 g/m². The owner should consciously accept that the cheapest Ø80-85 skirt is not the cheapest in the market.
- [low] Small items priced well below the reference or the market floor. kruk-laag-stapelbaar €27.95, against Outmate €37.50 (metal stool) and €42.50 (low stool with wooden seat) and Statafelshop €47.80: -25%. Its price_ref note wrongly says no reference exists. led-lichtslang in colour €7.95/m against Outmate €15/m: -47%. stoelhoes-stretch €5.95–6.95 against Statafelshop's chair stretch cover at €20.51 (our note cites €4–8 market). wijnvat-set €649 against €775: -16%. pakket-24-barkrukken-met-transportkar €1,059 against Outmate's comparable 24 + trolley pack at €1,299: -18%. Biertafelset sets -18 to -21% against Outmate; within Statafelshop's band.
- [low] Easy-up wall sets come in one layout. Outmate sells two set types (Type 1 / Type 2) for the long 3×6 and 4×8 tents in polyester and heavy-duty PVC. We have one zijwandenset-easy-up layout per size.
- [low] Buizenframe colourways that Outmate stocks and we do not: (1) an anthracite roof with anthracite-white walls on the compleet line, where ours offers only Wit, Grijs-wit and Antraciet-wit; (2) Zwart on PVC 550 Premium, which Outmate stocks in 3×6 and 4×8; (3) mixed roof/wall combinations (grey roof with grey-white walls, green roof with green-white walls).
- [low] Loose items Outmate sells that we lack: (1) single storage bags by size (6 sizes, €17–19); we sell only the opbergtassenset; (2) a 50 cm extension piece for the overkapping wall (€29); (3) a complete stretch tent made to measure, where we sell only the cloth; (4) loose bases for the square, terrace and wobble-free tables; (5) a 110 cm tilt-top statafel and an Ø60 statafel size.
- [low] Lighting depth. LED lamps missing: cold white, U-shaped filament, 3 W spiral filament, coloured filament lamps and a set of 5 coloured filament lamps. Lighting parts missing: lamp shade, cross and straight link connectors for prikkabels, 2×2,5 mm² cable per metre, wifi plug, socket switch set with remote.
- [low] Smaller depth gaps. Barrier ropes: no rope-style cord, and 8 velvet cord colours against about 11 at Outmate. Seating: no rattan bench or rattan stacking chair; Statafelshop's rattan line runs €142–290, and we have only barkruk-pe-rotan.
- [low] Heater covers. The single beschermhoes-terrasheater is offered as the add-on on the flame heater as well, while its own spec says it fits paddenstoelheaters. The lounge heater has no cover. Outmate sells a separate cover per heater model at €19.50–27.50. Fix: add a cover variant per model, or drop the flame heater from past_op.
- [low] bedrukte-zijwand-easy-up past_op leaves out easy-up-partytent-professional-2x2 and -5x5. The product sells 2 m and 5 m lengths for exactly those tents, and its copy says the 2 m wall fits every side of the 2 × 2 m. As a result the 'Past op' filter and the compatibility and parts lists on those two tents are incomplete.
- [low] Copy and label drift. loper-per-meter copy says the runner comes in seven colours, but the product offers 10. The main-menu label 'Breed stapelbaar 180 × 70' points to statafel-breed-stapelbaar, which also sells 140 × 70.
- [low] Downloads (productblad and opbouwhandleiding) are missing on 15 core tables. These are the HDPE rectangular and round folding tables, both bankettafels, klaptafel-hout-vormen, the conferentie- and examentafel, the folding table with benches, and every terrastafel. The Premium full colour easy-up has none either, while its plain twins do. Outmate has downloads on only some of its tables, so this is a 'lead further' item.
- [low] Owner action, not a catalog defect. catalog/stock.csv has no counts, so every product card and PDP reads 'Leverbaar over 2–3 weken'. Outmate shows a next-working-day dispatch line on in-stock items. This is correct by design (the brief forbids invented stock), but it is the largest remaining conversion gap against the reference at launch. Only 19 of 243 products carry a confirmed levertijd.
- [low] Supply claims that go beyond what the reference stocks: 7–8 buizenframe colourways per size (Outmate stocks 1–5), Professional 2×2 in 3 colours and 5×5 in 4 (Outmate: black only), waterpoten tent in 3×4,5 and 3×6 (Outmate: 3×3 only), 8 inflatable frame colours, and stretch tents in Wit and Zwart. All are listed in docs/claims-to-confirm.md. They must be confirmed with the supplier before launch, or the values removed.
